A freshly staged dropper is unknown to every reputation feed by design: it has only just been built or served, so nobody has submitted it yet. In that context an unknown hash is weakly suspicious and worth pivoting on, especially when it came from a live scan of an untrusted page.

Re-check later: reputation for a genuinely new sample often appears within hours of first submission. Detonating the sample in a sandbox is the next step that will actually produce evidence.
