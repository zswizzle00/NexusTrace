Using NexusTrace without an account

You can use every lookup without signing in. To run and protect the site, each request is logged with its time, your IP address and country, your browser's user agent, the page or tool you used, and the response status. The log records which tool you used, not what you looked up. It is used to spot abuse and to understand how the site is used, and it is never sold or shared.

What you look up

To answer a lookup, NexusTrace sends the indicator you entered (an IP address, domain, URL, hash, or similar) to the threat intelligence providers listed on the result page. Each result page names the providers it contacted and what it sent them. Those providers handle the data under their own privacy policies.

Scans, e-mail analyses, and other saved results are stored so that you can come back to them. You can delete a saved result from its own result page at any time.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. When you sign in with GitHub or Google, NexusTrace stores only:

  • your user ID at that provider, and your display name
  • when you created the account and when you last signed in
  • any API key you choose to save, encrypted

NexusTrace never sees your password, and it doesn't ask GitHub or Google for your e-mail address. A sign-in lasts 24 hours and uses a single session cookie. Deleting your account on the Account page removes it and every key you saved, right away.

How long data is kept

Saved results and the request log are deleted automatically after 30 days. Account records are kept until you delete your account.

Cookies

NexusTrace uses only the cookies it needs to work: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and protects forms against cross-site request forgery. There are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Hosting

The site is served through Cloudflare, which processes requests on the way to NexusTrace under its own privacy policy.

Contact
